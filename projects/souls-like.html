Overview

The Souls-like prototype is a work in progress building toward a boss encounter. So far it covers enemy AI, animation, and a finished arena.

The goal of this project is to create a polished Souls-like boss fight inspired by Dark Souls III's Nameless King. Working through the AI and player systems has allowed me to build strong familiarity with UE5 AI tools and animation workflows—the two most critical pillars of a memorable boss encounter. I chose this as a long-term project because video game AI has always fascinated me, and Soulsborne boss fights are a standout example of pairing expressive animations with dynamic decision logic to create a challenging, rewarding experience.

Systems I built

  • Melee and mage enemy archetypes with multiple attacks based on player interactions
  • Sight, sound, and damage perception for enemies, including target-forgetting behavior
  • Decision-tree and pathing logic
  • Attack triggers and attack animations
  • Animation blueprints with custom attack sets
  • Volumetric fog, dynamic weather, Niagara particle systems, and rain

Design decisions

Enemy perception and decision logic

Decision
Implemented a multi-sense perception setup combining sight, sound, and damage stimuli.
Why
Pairing dynamic enemy reactions with patrol routes required enemies to respond to more than just visual contact. Sight and damage are expected, but tying sound events to specific animations and player actions forces the player to stay mindful of their movement and timing, not just line of sight.
Tradeoffs
Currently, when a single stimulus triggers, the enemy moves directly to that stimulus location. Next, I plan to integrate UE5's Environment Query System (EQS) to build a proper investigation state where enemies actively search the surrounding area.

Arena scale and mood

Decision
Established a dark, stormy atmosphere for the environment.
Why
The main challenge was tuning the skysphere and UE5 volumetric cloud systems so lightning flashes convincingly through the storm clouds.
Tradeoffs
To fully tie the environment into the Storm King boss theme, the final step is adding damaging lightning strikes via Niagara particle systems so the weather interacts directly with the player during combat.
